1. **SplatAnything: Gaussian Spacing Normalization for Feed-forward 3DGS across Arbitrary Scenes** - 👍 60
   - 기관: Zhejiang University881
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.08264)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08264)
   - Abstract: We identify a key limitation of existing feed-forward 3DGS methods: Gaussian attributes are typically predicted in a radial-distance-normalized space that canonicalizes a global radial statistic of the reconstructed geometry, while the spacing between neighboring Gaussian centers can still vary substantially across scene scales and camera configurations. This mismatch induces systematic shifts in Gaussian-scale targets, leading to ambiguous supervision. To address this, we propose SplatAnything with Gaussian Spacing Normalization (GSNorm), which canonicalizes a robust scene-level statistic of Gaussian spacing to provide a more consistent spatial reference for Gaussian prediction. To rigorously assess our method, we further establish a systematic benchmark spanning diverse datasets, input sparsity levels, and challenging extrapolated viewpoints. Extensive experiments demonstrate that SplatAnything achieves state-of-the-art performance, with particularly strong gains under substantial variations in scene scale and viewpoint.

2. **P^2-MOPD: Accelerating Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation via Parameter Priors** - 👍 44
   - 기관: StepFun36
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.03953)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03953)
   - Abstract: Multi-teacher on-policy distillation consolidates expert capabilities by supervising student-generated trajectories, whereas task arithmetic offers a training-free alternative through linear parameter combinations. We view the linear combination of student and expert parameters as a single differentiable model and propose P$^2$-MOPD, which jointly optimizes the mixing coefficients and the student weights under the same on-policy distillation objective. Unlike task arithmetic, which stays within the span of the frozen student and expert parameters for any choice of merging coefficients, P$^2$-MOPD keeps the student trainable, so the experts act as parameter priors without confining it to this span. Across four domains at 1.7B and 4B scales, P$^2$-MOPD achieves faster and more balanced recovery than MOPD from the same initialization, surpassing its final average recovery in half the training steps. At the end of training, our 1.7B model reaches 94.7% average recovery, versus 80.9% for MOPD.

3. **LongBanana: An Expert-Verified Benchmark for Long-Context Multi-Reference Image Synthesis** - 👍 28
   - 기관: Kolors Team, Kuaishou Technology22
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.06856)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06856)
   - Abstract: We introduce LongBanana, to our knowledge the first expert-designed and expert-verified benchmark for long-context multi-reference image synthesis in the age of agent-driven image generation. The task requires retrieving dispersed visual evidence, rejecting distractors, binding entities and relations, and composing one coherent final image from multiple references. LongBanana contains 90 carefully designed tasks organized by a 3-by-3 taxonomy; each task includes about 32 reference images, 20 constraints, and 50 evaluation items, yielding 4,670 expert-specified atomic evaluation items in total. Its graph-grounded evaluation design instantiates each task with reference images and an entity-relation graph, then converts node, edge, and whole-graph obligations into checklist items for Content Fidelity, Relational Binding, and Global Constraint Satisfaction. Reviewed expert labels include a 15% double-labeled subset with 89.4% exact agreement and 0.83 quadratic weighted kappa. We further introduce LongBanana-Gen, an agent-harness framework for long-horizon image generation; experiments show that minimal agent harnesses improve over direct generation, while a ReAct-Plan-Exec-Reflect (RPR) workflow yields harness-dependent gains by strengthening evidence grounding and repair. Together, LongBanana provides an auditable testbed for measuring dense visual evidence use and a shared target for developing stronger agent-driven image-generation systems. Code and data are available at the project page: this https URL .

4. **PCBRouteAgent: How Close Are LLM Agents to Becoming PCB Routing Engineers?** - 👍 24
   - 기관: ·5 authors147
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.08175)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08175)
   - Abstract: Automating printed circuit board (PCB) routing requires coordinating board-level connectivity decisions with geometric execution under strict design rules. Although recent agents can interact with native routing tools, reliable routing on complex boards remains challenging. We present PCBRouteAgent, a multimodal PCB routing agent inspired by how human engineers coordinate global strategy with local execution. A Global Routing Planner combines board images and structured electrical and geometric information to specify connection topology, routing order, corridors, and layer transitions. A Detailed Routing Executor realizes these guides through KiCad's native routing engine and performs bounded local recovery. Unresolved failures are returned as structured feedback, enabling targeted guide revisions while preserving accepted routes. We evaluate PCBRouteAgent on 247 synthetic and real-world boards from four PCBWorld-Bench test splits. It achieves clean-pass rates of 100%, 94.9%, 100%, and 40% on D2, D3-A, D3-B, and D3-C, respectively. On the three real-world subsets, PCBRouteAgent outperforms the best evaluated baseline in clean-pass rate by 13.9, 36, and 10 percentage points, respectively.

5. **Confucius4-R2T2: A Stable-Prefix Streaming ASR Model for Real Real-Time Transcription** - 👍 24
   - 기관: Netease Youdao8711
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.06043)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06043)
   - Abstract: Streaming speech recognition is increasingly consumed by machines, such as translators and voice agents, that act on each token as it appears. A token that is later revised costs wasted work or a wrong action. In this work, we present Confucius4-R2T2 (short for Real Real-Time Transcription), a stable-prefix streaming ASR model that never modifies its earlier output while maintaining high accuracy and low latency. R2T2 is obtained by post-training Qwen3-ASR-1.7B on 1,500 hours of Chinese and English audio while freezing its pre-trained audio encoder and projector. The adaptation is based on the concept of Longest Stable Prefix (LSP), a new notion that we introduce in this work. We prove that LSP is monotone under the prefix order, consistent with the full-audio output, and maximal among labels that equal a whole earlier output. Learning from LSP labels converts a full-context model into a stable-prefix streaming one without any architectural change. Extensive evaluations show that R2T2 brings streaming recognition close to the base model's full-context quality while delivering stable text with low emission lag. Its gains extend beyond the post-training languages, demonstrating a practical path from full-context ASR to stable streaming. We open-source the model weights and code at this https URL and this https URL .

6. **Beneath the Surface: What We Overlook When We Talk About Agent Evolution** - 👍 22
   - 기관: University of Science and Technology of China2
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.07624)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07624)
   - Abstract: Agent harness evolution enables recursive self-improvement (RSI) without updating model weights. We examine whether higher benchmark scores yield reliable, efficient, and transferable improvement. We evaluate four methods across harnesses, general and coding tasks. Repeated evaluations, controlled agent expansion, component interventions, and admission replays reveal recurring patterns. All sixteen evolved agents gain previously uncovered tasks while losing previously covered ones. In one general-task case, reliability rises by 5.2 points despite losing ten previously covered tasks. Most reliability gains also increase deployment cost. Specific changes usually outperform generic reminders, but their benefits can change when combined. The highest-scoring agent does not yield the largest mean improvement in any of our four pools, and stricter admission can discard useful alternatives. Transfer selectively preserves gains: coverage usually falls on the general-task target across both harness types and paradigms, but rises on the coding target with the outer-layer-only harness. These findings motivate preservation-aware evaluation, tested decision rules, and explicit applicability conditions. As a constructive follow-up, EvoAudit turns these diagnostics into revisions of select, analyze, mutate, and gate, achieving the best performance.

7. **LLMs Don't Know What They Know: Benchmarking Temporal Self-Knowledge for Financial Backtesting** - 👍 21
   - 기관: The Fin AI2
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.06808)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06808)
   - Abstract: Financial backtests of large language models (LLMs) are meaningful only if the model did not already know the outcomes it is asked to predict. Practitioners guard against this by trusting a vendor's stated knowledge cutoff, by asking the model what it knows, or by instructing it to answer as of a past date. To verify whether any of these safeguards can be trusted, we introduce FinDendro, a benchmark of 2,226 dated U.S. facts from 1999 to 2026, covering monthly macroeconomic series, month-end stock prices and Federal Reserve decisions. Each fact yields four prompts: a value question, Recall and Predict questions that pair each candidate value with a claim of knowing or guessing without a declared date, and a Predict question with a historical declared date. Controls for persistence and dominant outcomes keep chance accuracy near 0.27. Results show that for most frontier models, self-reports understate knowledge. GPT-5 answers 76% of controlled facts correctly but claims to know 38%, and answers 61% of the facts it says it does not know. Knowledge claims can persist beyond the measured knowledge boundary, by up to seven quarters, and a declared date removes most of the claims of prior knowledge while leaving accuracy unchanged. Backtests inside a model's training window therefore cannot be certified leak-free by self-report, instruction or stated cutoff. These findings motivate behavioral measurement of temporal knowledge boundaries, and FinDendro provides a benchmark and procedure for selecting backtest periods based on demonstrated factual knowledge.

8. **Fast and Exact Relative-Position Attention via Structure-Aware Kernels** - 👍 19
   - 기관: Institute for Computer Science, Artificial intelligence and Technology1
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.01182)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01182)
   - Abstract: Relative-position attention mechanisms offer compelling alternatives to RoPE across various transformer architectures, delivering superior performance in domains such as audio. These mechanisms typically modify attention scores by adding content-position or bias-position terms. Unlike RoPE, which encodes position via per-token rotations directly on the queries and keys, relative-position terms generally require pairwise offset-dependent computation or lookups, complicating their integration into fused attention kernels and introducing severe memory, bandwidth, and compute overheads as sequences scale. To address this bottleneck, we propose an exact attention algorithm that incorporates additive relative-position terms into FlashAttention-style tiled computation. Our method (1) uses offset-aware tiling to avoid redundant indexing and memory accesses, (2) computes only the necessary key/query-relation products and reuses them across token pairs in both forward and backward passes, and (3) uses offset-based gradient sharding and aggregation, eliminating atomic contention. We provide a unified, efficient Triton implementation across diverse relative-position attention mechanisms and evaluate it against naive and specialized implementations. For 8K-token sequences, our method speeds up the forward (backward) pass by 1.8-6.4$\times$ (2.0-21$\times$) and reduces peak memory up to 2.9-128$\times$ (1.4-54$\times$) over the strongest baseline for each mechanism, enabling long-context end-to-end transcription that is 1.8$\times$ faster and uses 2.4$\times$ less memory. Our method thus provides a drop-in, hardware-efficient solution for scaling relative-position attention transformers.

9. **EvoWorld: Towards Out-of-Sight World Evolution in Generative Video Models** - 👍 19
   - 기관: ·8 authors221
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.06748)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06748)
   - Abstract: Generative video models are increasingly used as visual world simulators, yet their primary strength lies in rendering plausible pixels rather than reasoning about how a world should evolve. Coherent world evolution requires more than visual fidelity: when an event occurs, a model must infer its consequences for the entities in the world and then render the resulting state. For example, after a wild beast appears, the model should not only depict the wild beast, but also infer whether a nearby person should flee, take cover, or fight back based on the realized scene. Existing diffusion-based video models lack an explicit mechanism for maintaining world state and reasoning over such event-conditioned consequences, often producing visually plausible but behaviorally static or causally inconsistent outputs. To make this reasoning gap concrete and measurable, we propose a new problem, Out-of-Sight Causal Evolution, which requires a model to render an event, reason about its consequences for the evolving world state, and reveal a causally plausible outcome as the world continues to evolve, even out of sight. As a baseline reference solution, we introduce EvoWorld, a brain-renderer collaboration framework in which a vision-language model serves as the world brain: it observes generated segments, maintains an evolving account of the world, and reasons about plausible state transitions, while a segment-autoregressive video renderer realizes them visually. We further introduce EvoWorld-Data and EvoWorld-Bench for training and evaluating event realization, causal reactions, and revisit consistency. Experiments show that EvoWorld substantially improves event-conditioned causal evolution over recent models while preserving visual quality and consistency, establishing a reference point for reasoning-guided, autonomous world evolution beyond the current field of view.

   - Abstract: Recent years have witnessed remarkable progress in video generation in terms of visual quality and temporal consistency. However, generating videos with precise spatial structure, coherent motion dynamics, and controllable scene composition remains challenging. In this paper, we introduce Video-Coder, the first attempt to train a code-driven video generation agent, which autonomously writes and executes code to construct a dynamic 3D scene (Draft Video) that provides structure and motion signals to downstream video generators. Experiments show that Video-Coder achieves significant gains, improving Minimax-H3 by approximately 7 points on PhyGenBench and 24 points on VBench-2.0. We hope this work can serve as an open foundation for coding agents in video generation, and we fully open-source our data, models, and code
