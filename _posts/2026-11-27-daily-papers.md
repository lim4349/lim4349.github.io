1. **Stable Gated DeltaNet-2: Understanding and Mitigating Recurrent State Instability** - 👍 101
   - 기관: MemTensor2
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.34051)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34051)
   - Abstract: Linear attention offers an efficient solution for long-sequence modeling via linear complexity and fixed-size recurrent states. Gated DeltaNet-2 (GDN-2) introduces independent channel-wise control over erasure and writing, enhancing state-update expressiveness and in-context retrieval, yet it suffers from training failures during long-context training. We trace these failures to the asymmetry between reading and modification directions during erasure, which yields non-normal erase operators whose directional gains accumulate across tokens, triggering exponential state growth even without new writes. To resolve this, we propose \model{}, employing symmetric erasure to ensure non-expansive state propagation and decay-regulated residual writing to preserve writing flexibility. Theoretically, we establish an $O(\sqrt{T})$ upper bound on recurrent state growth while retaining chunkwise parallel computation. Experiments on ~1.6B parameter models show that \model{} achieves stable training alongside improved long-context retrieval. Compared to GDN-2, it boosts the 128K average RULER score by 6.80 points in the recurrent setting and average accuracy across nine downstream tasks by 0.62 points in the hybrid setting.

2. **OS-Omni: Benchmarking Generalist Computer-Using Agents on Long-Horizon Workflows Across Platforms** - 👍 94
   - 기관: Touchstone AI292
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.34232)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34232)
   - Abstract: Computer-using agents are increasingly expected to complete realistic user goals rather than isolated GUI actions. However, existing benchmarks typically isolate web, mobile, or desktop environments, leaving it unclear whether a single generalist agent can execute long-horizon workflows that span multiple operating systems and persistent application states. We introduce OS-Omni, a live cross-platform benchmark and execution environment for evaluating computer-using agents across Windows, Ubuntu, macOS, iOS, Android, and Web environments. OS-Omni is released in versioned waves of 300 tasks each; this paper focuses on the first release, v1, which contains 48 tasks per platform and 12 cross-platform workflows that pair a desktop with an Android phone. OS-Omni provides reproducible initialization, platform-specific adapters, trajectory logging, and executable final-state evaluators, and includes controlled service-backed applications for workflows involving account, cart, budget, calendar, message, and other persistent state. Human studies show that OS-Omni tasks require 67.54 interaction steps, substantially exceeding OSWorld references of 15.0 steps. On v1, the strongest evaluated agent, Claude Opus 5.5, reaches 68.5% average success, trailing the 77.3% human reference by 8.8 points; the gap is uneven across platforms, with frontier models exceeding humans on Linux but trailing them by more than 25 points on iOS and macOS. These results indicate that current agents remain far from platform-robust, long-horizon computer use, especially when success requires sustained state tracking and durable final-state changes.

3. **What a Forecasting Leaderboard Can Establish: Resolution Reporting for Zero-Shot Time-Series Models** - 👍 35
   - 기관: ·2 authors71
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.31619)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31619)
   - Abstract: A foundation model for time series is pretrained once and then asked to forecast series it has never seen, so the evidence for it is its score on held-out datasets. Such datasets are scarce, and a few public leaderboards carry most comparisons, yet none of them states how small a difference its datasets can detect. We call that quantity the resolution of a board and introduce Vernier, a tool that computes it from the files a board already publishes. Vernier also reports which entries a board cannot separate from its leader and whether its order changes when unrelated parties submit. GIFT-Eval resolves 6.8 rank places, and its displayed neighbours sit 0.91 apart. The ten places of its top ten hold fewer than two resolvable steps. fev-bench, Chronos Benchmark II and TIME show the same pattern: their resolution is 3.6 to 19 times the gap between neighbours. With each of its 2,554 series as an independent source, BOOM is the one board that resolves its top ten. Below the resolution the order depends on who else submitted: across four boards, rules that rank against the pool reversed 43 top-ten pairs with neither entry rerun, in one update in seven. Boards also do not record how much history a model receives. On 70 configurations, shortening it raised the error of three of four open models, two of them by 15%. 4. **ProxyGenBench: Benchmarking Proxy-Guided Video Generation** - 👍 35
   - 기관: Tencent22
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.34457)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34457)
   - Abstract: Recent video generators produce realistic videos, yet controlling how scenes unfold remains challenging. Structural proxies, such as animated 3D blockouts, guide generation by specifying scene layout, motion, camera behavior, and event order. However, these coarse inputs leave details unspecified and may contain artifacts that should not be reproduced. Successful proxy-guided generation must therefore Preserve intended structure, Complete missing content and correct proxy artifacts, and Bind text, image, and audio conditions to the right entities and events. We introduce ProxyGenBench to study these interacting requirements, with 312 evaluation cases from 60 scenes across three scene tasks and controlled variations in proxy fidelity, text detail, and multimodal inputs. We evaluate five video generators using verified, case-specific checklists. Experiments show that proxies improve spatiotemporal control, but greater proxy detail can coincide with poorer completion, while strong structural adherence does not ensure correct binding.

5. **StateWeaver: Multi-Agent Coordination of Story-State Transitions for Long-Horizon Video Generation** - 👍 23
   - 기관: ·9 authors381
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.34290)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34290)
   - Abstract: Modern video foundation models generate high-quality short clips, but preserving long-video consistency and correctly depicting event-driven state changes remain challenging. Existing agentic workflows face these challenges because continual planning and repair accumulate context, while reference updates remain limited. We introduce StateWeaver, which combines Active States and Hierarchical Collaboration to connect review-driven visual reference updates with cross-shot state inheritance. Active States translate state discrepancies identified during review into concrete visual asset requirements that guide reference construction, validation, and regeneration, preserving identity and already-correct content while repairing target attributes. Confirmed generation results are linked to the corresponding asset states, story time, and reference versions for verification and reuse by subsequent shots. Hierarchical Collaboration isolates execution contexts by task, clip, and repair round, and uses hierarchical memory to organize this process across multiple sets. Across multiple benchmarks, StateWeaver outperforms existing methods and produces long videos with sustained cross-shot consistency under predefined and autonomously planned storyboards.

6. **ShowNow: Fine-Grained Speculative Streaming for Responsive World Models** - 👍 19
   - 기관: Show Lab32
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.34693)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34693)
   - Abstract: Interactive world models must generate video continuously while responding promptly to user actions. Real-time throughput alone leaves input latency unresolved: coarse generation blocks delay action updates, and generation after input adds work to the response path. We introduce ShowNow, a fine-grained speculative streaming framework that uses computation available during playback to prepare action-conditioned futures before input arrives. Single-latent commitment enables frequent decisions, while configurable preparation covers one to eight actions. We construct a matched multi-future dataset to train alternative continuations from a common state; shared-history tree execution and fused commitment make their preparation efficient. On a single H100, our 5B model sustains 480p generation at 16 fps with eight prepared futures. In ViZDoom, the complete ShowNow deployment reduces median input-to-display latency from 620 to 208 ms relative to the native-block SolarWM-FT deployment. Matched-policy experiments reduce restart empty ticks from 1.56% to 0.09% (94%) and improve on-time responses to action changes. These results establish complementary roles for fine-grained commitment and advance preparation, with measured trade-offs in visual fidelity and long-horizon consistency. ShowNow turns playback-time computation into responsive visual interaction. Project: this https URL .

7. **DRBench Pro: A Knowledge-Centric Benchmark for Professional Deep Research Agents** - 👍 15
   - 기관: Zhejiang University191
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.34852)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34852)
   - Abstract: Deep research agents gather information from multiple sources and synthesize it into reports on complex, open-ended questions. Existing benchmarks assess report quality along several dimensions, but lack a dedicated knowledge-centric evaluation of which research directions a report covers and how deeply it explores them. We introduce DRBench Pro, a benchmark that makes structured knowledge coverage the primary evaluation target. Our automated pipeline builds local knowledge trees from Wikipedia and uses sampled subgraphs to generate research queries and evidence-grounded rubrics. Each rubric corresponds to a relation in the tree, preserving the structure of the knowledge being evaluated. Agents receive only the research query. This structure allows us to measure breadth across major research directions and depth along related knowledge chains, with their geometric mean giving overall coverage. The benchmark includes 144 cases across 24 domains and 44,102 rubrics. Evaluating 16 deep research agents reveals different strengths in breadth and depth, with the best-performing agent reaching 57.04% overall coverage. Rubric judgments agree closely with human annotations, and agent rankings remain stable across the tested metric configurations.

8. **Solve Vision with Code: Coding Agents Are Stronger Visual Reasoners Than Video Models** - 👍 9
   - 기관: Carnegie Mellon University1
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.34813)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34813)
   - Abstract: Video generation models are increasingly evaluated as visual reasoners: given a first frame and a natural-language prompt, can the model produce the video in which the specified task is carried out? The paradigm is considered advantageous because the reasoning is native to the visual medium, unfolding directly in the frames rather than through language or code. Whether this native route is in fact the more reliable path to visual reasoning has not been tested against a symbolic alternative under the same conditions. We therefore pose the same tasks, with identical inputs, output specification and evaluator, to both media: rather than denoising pixels, a coding agent writes a program that renders the answer video, which an unmodified rule-based evaluator scores. On VBVR-Pro-Bench (Xu et al., 2026), a 500-instance suite spanning five cognitive categories with symbolic ground truth, the agent receives the same first frame and prompt as the video model, with no access to ground truth, metadata or generation models, and one attempt per instance. Three closed-model agents (Codex with gpt-6-astra, Claude Code with Fable 5.1 and Gemini CLI with Gemini 3.1 Pro) score 0.923, 0.894 and 0.893, each more than 0.22 above the strongest video model. Among 23 runnable open-weight models, the best, GLM-5, reaches 0.572, above all video models except the RL-trained one. Together, we show that for tasks whose solutions can be computed from a first frame and a prompt, program synthesis is currently a substantially more reliable route to visual reasoning than video generation. We further distinguish depictive programs, which navigate the image to recover a transferable scheme, from propositional programs, which operate on hard-coded values, revealing how visual reasoning unfolds in agent-written programs and what it implies for its nature of reasoning in general.

9. **Boundary-Aware Policy Learning for Agent-Orchestrated Robot Manipulation** - 👍 6
   - 기관: Sage Physical AI1
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.32238)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32238)
   - Abstract: Agent-orchestrated robot systems combine the general reasoning capability of multimodal agents with the precise physical control of learned visuomotor policies. Yet such systems often fail at the moment of handoff: a policy is reliable only from a limited set of start states, while the agent may invoke it outside this region. We call this region the policy's handoff boundary. Existing whole-task policies typically have handoff boundaries that are narrow and difficult for an agent to characterize, making reliable coordination challenging. We propose Boundary-Aware Policy Learning, which explicitly shapes and then expands the handoff boundary of a visuomotor policy. First, Boundary-Explicit Training fine-tunes the policy on contact segments with diverse start poses, yielding a wide and well-defined boundary that can be communicated to the agent through a simple handoff protocol. Second, Boundary-Expanding Self-Improvement retrains the policy on curated successful system rollouts, extending the boundary toward earlier stages of each task and allowing the agent to hand over sooner with fewer language-model calls. Across RoboTwin 2.0 and RoboDojo, our system outperforms the strongest baseline by over 26 percentage points, reaching 98.4% success in randomized RoboTwin 2.0 scenes, while further reducing its API cost and execution time by 41.3% and 37.5%, respectively. These results suggest that the key to robust agent-policy orchestration lies not only in improving either component individually, but also in explicitly shaping the boundary between them.

10. **When an AI agent makes a mistake in a database, what decides the damage?** - 👍 3
   - 기관: LibreDB21
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.30483)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30483)
   - Abstract: AI agents now run statements against production databases, and several of the incidents that followed deleted production data, which came back only through backups or the provider's help. We ask what decides the damage when such an agent makes a mistake: the model, a guard layer in front of the database, or the database engine itself. The study has two preregistered layers. In Layer A, a frozen library of 22 canonical mistakes is applied by script, with no model involved, to four relational engines under five permission arms and two recovery arms, followed by an engine-native recovery drill (792 cells; 874 drill runs including 10-minute repeats). In Layer B, a minimal tool-calling agent driven by six small open-weight models (4B to 12B parameters, plus one 35B mixture-of-experts model with about 3B active, served locally) works on tasks that contain hazards across nine engines: PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQLite, ClickHouse, Apache Cassandra, MongoDB, Valkey, Apache Kafka and OpenSearch (9,468 trials). A secondary run added MariaDB, Redis and Elasticsearch (2,916 trials); each behaved like its closest core engine. Out of the box, the engines differ less in what a mistake destroys than in what they let you undo. Three of the seven preregistered hypotheses met their decision rule: H2 (for one of nine damage classes) and H3 largely by construction of the arms, and H4 only under its original measure. Four did not; one of them (H5) was uninformative, because in its task the agents never found the credential. The post-hoc analyses (S1-S13) are exploratory and uncorrected for multiple comparisons; on PostgreSQL, MySQL, OpenSearch and ClickHouse the preregistered outcome and original-row loss disagree about G1. We report all of them, with every deviation from the preregistration and its timing.
