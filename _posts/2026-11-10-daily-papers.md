1. **Learning Heterogeneous Tactile Representation for General Dexterous Manipulation** - 👍 92
   - 기관: Robbyant1371
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.12806)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12806)
   - Abstract: Tactile sensing is an indispensable modality for contact-rich dexterous manipulation, a capability that hints at the ultimate frontiers of embodied intelligence. However, commercially available tactile sensors are still at a relatively early stage of development, with each type of sensor offering irreplaceable strengths alongside its own drawbacks. A key challenge is thus to free tactile understanding from sensor-specific physics, so that embodied models can effectively leverage heterogeneous tactile data. To this end, we develop LingBot-Touch, a tactile foundation model characterized by the following properties. (i) Our model yields a unified representation across different tactile sensors (i.e., piezoresistive, magnetic, and visuotactile sensors), and is easily extensible to new sensor types. (ii) The resulting tactile tokens are plug-and-play: they can be seamlessly integrated into policy models across paradigms, from diffusion policy to vision-language-action (VLA) models such as $\pi_{0.5}$ and LingBot-VLA, consistently enhancing manipulation dexterity. (iii) Extensive experiments, spanning simulation and real-robot deployments across multiple hardware platforms, demonstrate that our tactile representation substantially benefits complex manipulation tasks while maintaining strong computational efficiency. We believe the findings in this work demonstrate the potential of leveraging cross-sensor data to scale tactile learning, and offer encouraging insights for the design of future hardware and embodied models.

2. **WorldFoundry: Towards Unified Infrastructure for World Intelligence** - 👍 52
   - 기관: OpenEnvision3361
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.10771)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10771)
   - Abstract: Visual generative models increasingly blur the line between synthesizing observations and simulating environments. Yet visual plausibility alone does not constitute successful world modeling: compelling outputs may lack persistent state, physical dynamics, or causally consistent responses to interactions. We define a generative world model as a predictive dynamical system that maintains a persistent, history-conditioned representation of an environment, updates that representation in response to actions or interventions, and generates temporally coherent trajectories that reflect outcomes under observed and counterfactual interventions. From this definition, we derive an intelligence-oriented framework spanning perceptual grounding, persistent state modeling, controllable transformation, dynamics-aware prediction, and closed-loop interaction. We present WorldFoundry, open-source infrastructure for reproducible inference and evaluation across video, 3D and 4D, embodied action, and interactive world systems. WorldFoundry unifies model and benchmark discovery, runtime and asset preparation, execution orchestration, artifact management, and interactive visualization, while modality-specific adapters preserve native inputs, execution semantics, output representations, and evaluation protocols. Explicit manifests, durable artifact contracts, and evidence-aware scorecards separate execution readiness from evaluative claims, making each claim traceable to its supporting artifacts. The catalog contains 270 model entries, 74 benchmarks, and 62 registered metric entries, with readiness and execution evidence recorded separately. WorldFoundry enables reproducible comparison of persistent, controllable dynamics across heterogeneous systems, advancing the field from perceptual synthesis toward measurable world intelligence.

3. **Sage-Taiyi: A Metacognitive Architecture for Scientific Discovery** - 👍 50
   - 기관: Shanghai AI Laboratory101
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.12762)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12762)
   - Abstract: We introduce Sage-Taiyi, a metacognition-inspired architecture that uses accumulated scientific understanding to guide what to investigate next and how to investigate it. Through explicit monitoring and control, the system revises its understanding of evidence, assumptions, and unresolved questions, then uses these assessments to adapt research targets and select reusable reasoning methods. On Taiyi-Bench, an AI co-scientist ideation benchmark comprising 265 tasks from 50 papers across ten disciplines, Sage-Taiyi ranks first on all eight metrics of historical rediscovery and open-ended ideation, achieving an Overall score of 54.0 compared with 47.9 for the strongest baseline, AI Scientist-v2. It also performs well on two external scientific ideation benchmarks. Five expert-guided collaborations further connect its proposals to experimental and computational validation across chemistry, materials science, and engineering. In chemistry, reaction-condition optimization raises the yield of cobalt-catalyzed carbonylation from trace amounts to 84% and photocatalytic allylic amination from 13% to 69%. These results support metacognitive regulation as an approach to scientific ideation and its progression toward practical validation.

4. **Reframe: Observation-Aligned Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation for Agentic Visual Reasoning** - 👍 37
   - 기관: Nanjing University142
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.12886)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12886)
   - Abstract: Agentic reinforcement learning (RL) and multi-teacher on-policy distillation (MOPD) offer complementary routes to post-training. For visual agents, however, tool use presents two related challenges. Models may overlook useful tool-returned observations, while poorly grounded actions can limit the evidence available for subsequent distillation. We introduce Reframe, a post-training framework that treats the observation state, rather than only the output distribution, as an object of alignment. First, Ground-Drop-GRPO (GD-GRPO) trains spatial and temporal specialists with evidence-aware rewards and stochastic removal of the original visual input, encouraging them to acquire grounded evidence and use the returned observations. Second, Observation-Gated-MOPD (OG-MOPD) replaces deficient tool actions with canonical ones mid-rollout and resumes distillation from the repaired observation. The two stages complement each other, as learning to rely on tool-returned observations can help the policy benefit from observation repair. Neither stage requires a task-specific SFT cold start. Extensive experiments over 14 image and video benchmarks demonstrate that Reframe outperforms both Mix-RL and vanilla MOPD. Ablation results show that GD-GRPO increases observation dependence and tool gain compared with outcome-based GRPO.

5. **Flide: Accelerating Decision Inference with Exact State Forking** - 👍 31
   - 기관: National University of Singapore3442
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.42148)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.42148)
   - Abstract: Decision models such as Jev have emerged as a highly promising paradigm for LLM agent systems, providing lightweight and reliable categorical reasoning for rapid System One execution. However, existing generation-oriented inference engines fail to support these workloads effectively, suffering from severe prefix recomputation overheads or distorted output probabilities. To address this challenge, we propose Flide, an efficient and exact inference engine that decouples the logical tree structure of a decision request from its physical execution within a single flattened token stream. Through ForkAttention, Flide navigates request dependencies using GPU-resident indices across both standard attention and recurrent Gated DeltaNet layers, guaranteeing in exact arithmetic that every query branch evaluates identically to an isolated forward pass. By expressing branch topologies directly in GPU-resident metadata, Flide accommodates diverse request mixtures with a compact set of pre-recorded execution graphs while enabling expanding agent histories to resume from checkpoints saved in earlier passes. Extensive evaluations on public hybrid and decision models demonstrate that Flide answers queries up to 9.5 times faster than vLLM and SGLang while staying closest to a float32 reference in total variation distance. By unifying physical execution throughput with mathematical exactness, Flide establishes a principled foundation for scalable, latency-critical decision intelligence in agent systems.

6. **ReproBridge: Building Reproducibility into ML Research Artifacts** - 👍 29
   - 기관: Google1
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.08452)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08452)
   - Abstract: Reproducing machine learning experiments requires reconstructing procedures from information scattered across papers, released code, and supporting artifacts. For coding agents, reproduction success can therefore depend on their ability to fill in missing details, rather than on the released artifacts alone. We ask whether preserving recovered procedures and decisions improves subsequent independent reproduction. We define a standardized artifact design comprising a refined codebase, environment setup, experiment launchers, and a reproduction guide, and introduce ReproBridge, an agent workflow for constructing these refined reproducible artifacts. ReproBridge-Bench provides 1,029 tasks from 387 experiments across 36 papers, anchored by five human-verified reference scores per task. Agents attempt tasks in fresh sessions with raw code or refined artifacts; a separate Auditor checks execution evidence. Across four evaluated models, refined artifacts raise mean reproduction success from 76.4% to 92.6% and reduce downstream API spending by 19.7% on average. The cross-model success-rate spread narrows from 21.6 to 3.9 percentage points, with all four models achieving mean success rates above 90%. Removing any component reduces success in the ablation study, with the largest losses from code and environment removal. These results suggest that preserving execution knowledge helps even models with lower raw-code success reproduce experiments more reliably and at lower API cost.

7. **Longtail-SWE: High-Quality SWE Tasks for Underrepresented Languages** - 👍 19
   - 기관: Nebius02
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.10304)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10304)
   - Abstract: Challenging repository-level benchmarks remain limited for many programming language ecosystems, where filtering scarce candidates with ambiguous descriptions or defective tests can discard valuable engineering problems. We introduce Longtail-SWE, a benchmark of 202 issue-resolution tasks across 13 languages with limited coverage in existing repository-level benchmarks: Clojure, Dart, Elixir, Erlang, F#, Haskell, Julia, Kotlin, Lua, OCaml, R, Scala, and Swift. Its construction uses a solver--judge--repairer pipeline: a solver attempts each candidate task, and a judge reviews the task together with the solver's trajectory and test results to guide revisions to tests and task descriptions. These revisions aim to correct tests that reject valid alternative implementations or accept incorrect solutions while retaining challenging tasks and preserving their intended requirements. We evaluate nine model configurations, including frontier models, and analyze task-solving performance, consistency across repeated attempts, and resource usage. We release all benchmark instances, executable environments, complete construction and evaluation tooling, and agent trajectories, together with an additional set of easier tasks to support evaluation of smaller models.

8. **InfraRSI: Harnessing Recursive Self-Improvement for Training Infrastructure** - 👍 14
   - 기관: TokenRhythm34
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.11558)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11558)
   - Abstract: While Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI) accelerates isolated algorithm and kernel synthesis, distributed LLM training depends on tightly coupled launch settings, operator kernels, and communication schedules, where single edits often hit out-of-memory (OOM) boundaries, compiler conflicts, measurement jitter, or silent numerical drift. We present InfraRSI, the agentic harness that turns open-ended human commands into verified training optimizations via a five-stage workflow: init locks editable scope and a fixed task contract over workload, loss, and timing invariants to prevent reward hacking, doctor profiles baseline phases and calibrates cluster noise, run co-optimizes launch knobs and code patches before re-verifying top candidates against the baseline and logging findings to a cross-hardware experience bank, and report and apply audit source paths, track failed-trial costs, and commit promoted changes. Rather than stepping greedily from a single incumbent, InfraRSI initializes from diverse experience-seeded configurations and fits global execution-time and censored peak-memory models across all trials, including OOMs. Using these models, it prices per-setting memory costs, revises miscalibrated LLM priors from live runs, and pairs memory-hungry optimizations with memory savers and verified code patches. Across four single- and multi-node dense and Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) LLM workloads, InfraRSI achieves a speedup of up to $2.75\times$ and lifts mean Model FLOPs Utilization (MFU) from $17.4\%$ to $30.0\%$, outperforming existing tuners and coding agents while transferring experience across GPUs. As it improves training infrastructure efficiency, InfraRSI forms a two-level Recursive Self-Improvement loop---revising priors online within runs and distilling experience across clusters---to push foundation model scaling.

9. **SIRIS: Can LLMs Tell When Simulation Errors Matter in AI-Driven Systems Research** - 👍 12
   - 기관: Microsoft Research2
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.10329)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10329)
   - Abstract: LLM agents increasingly drive systems research and rely on simulators to evaluate candidate designs at scale. Simulators are imperfect by design: their modeling limitations can distort predictions yet leave the recommended design correct, or change which design should be selected. We introduce Siris to test whether LLMs can tell these cases apart, judging from their systems understanding when a limitation invalidates the simulator's recommendation. By perturbing a simulator, Siris constructs workload pairs with the same candidates, limitation, and simulator recommendation. The perturbation changes predictions on both workloads, yet a reference simulator supports the recommendation on only one. Evaluating thirteen frontier LLMs on LLM serving design problems, we find that identifying a limitation does not ensure a correct choice, and even the strongest models sometimes abandon valid recommendations. Self-correction through a review turn helps these models restore valid choices but introduces new errors in several others. These findings motivate evaluating systems understanding through design decisions under imperfect simulators.

10. **How Much Training-Inference Mismatch Can On-Policy Distillation Tolerate?** - 👍 11
   - 기관: ·8 authors36
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.11450)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11450)
   - Abstract: On-policy distillation (OPD) trains a student language model on its own responses, guided by a fixed teacher. Generating these responses is expensive, so systems use quantized inference and asynchronous rollouts to reduce the cost. Both, however, introduce training-inference mismatch between the rollout engine and the model being trained. Such mismatch can destabilize reinforcement learning (RL), but its effects on OPD remain unclear. This paper investigates how much mismatch OPD tolerates, where its effects arise, and how training systems can use this tolerance. Measuring all mismatch sources on one scale, we examine quantization across three student-teacher pairs, with additional tests of weight staleness and injected noise on one pair. OPD tolerates substantial mismatch without meaningful loss of training quality but degrades at higher levels, revealing an empirical mismatch budget. Both the quality cost of mismatch and the mismatch introduced by quantization depend on the model, with some layers particularly sensitive to reduced precision. In controlled comparisons, OPD remains stable under rollout configurations that destabilize RL methods. Under weight noise, mismatch also shifts the prefixes reached during generation, introducing errors that per-token corrections cannot address. To put these findings into practice, we introduce a lightweight admission check that screens rollout configurations against a calibrated budget without requiring a full training run for each candidate. Model-specific FP8 recipes preserve training quality while providing capacity for at least $1.93\times$ as many KV-cache tokens as BF16.
