1. **dNaViT: Discrete Codes are General-Purpose Visual Representations** - 👍 28
   - 기관: LongCat1
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.12426)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12426)
   - Abstract: Discrete autoregressive modeling, notably through next-token prediction, has become a cornerstone of modern language models. Extending this paradigm to vision allows visual and language models to converge on a unified framework, thereby fostering shared methodology and infrastructure. This work investigates a central question: how far can a finite discrete vocabulary go in representing the richness of the visual world? We present dNaViT, a Discrete Native-resolution Vision Transformer that produces discrete visual primitives analogous to language tokens. Built upon a frozen pretrained encoder, dNaViT applies lightweight residual modulation with a dual-branch objective to inject reconstruction-aware cues into semantic features. By leveraging grouped product quantization, it scales discrete capacity without inflating spatial sequence lengths, while a balanced training recipe maintains both semantic and pixel-level information. Consequently, dNaViT functions as a versatile discrete visual tokenizer, enabling unified encoding across downstream tasks alongside high-fidelity image decoding. Extensive evaluations show that dNaViT matches or exceeds task-specific baselines across 12 visual understanding benchmarks, 4 generation benchmarks, and an image editing benchmark. These findings demonstrate that a compact discrete vocabulary can serve as a universal visual currency and unlock the full potential of fully unified, native multimodal methodology.

2. **World In-Context Learning for Agentic Robot Manipulation** - 👍 25
   - 기관: H-EmbodVis361
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.12885)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12885)
   - Abstract: Trained action policies write manipulation skill into network weights, tying what is learned to the distribution it is learned from. In-context learning (ICL) avoids this coupling but has not matched trained policies, because a demonstration recorded in another scene carries no feedback from the world the robot currently faces. We observe that what an agent learns in context is semantic, recording task steps and object roles rather than visual appearance, so experience gathered in one world transfers to another. Based on this insight, we propose World In-Context Learning (WICL), a paradigm in which a frozen model starts from a demonstration, rehearses the task in a world such as a simulator, and writes the feedback into its context rather than its weights. We build RoboScript, a modular infrastructure that implements WICL for agentic manipulation. From a single demonstration, RoboScript learns each task through rehearsal in simulation, freezes the learned knowledge as a Task Memory, and deploys the frozen memory on new scenes with data isolation that ensures every deployment input is available on a real robot. On all 50 tasks of RoboTwin 2.0, RoboScript reaches 83.4% mean success across clean and randomized settings with one demonstration per task and no gradient updates, exceeding the strongest trained action policy by over 20 points. The method generalizes across visual distributions, improves through reflection, scales with the foundation model, and transfers to a physical dual-arm robot through cross-embodiment memory adaptation. With a world in the loop, a robot can learn a manipulation task in its context as well as it learns one in its weights. All code is available at this https URL

3. **MMDM: Measuring Massive Multitask Decision Making** - 👍 23
   - 기관: The Chinese University of Hong Kong - Database Group11
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.14309)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14309)
   - Abstract: System-1 Decision Models directly make structured probabilistic decisions over query-specified options given a state in a single forward pass, without autoregressively generating reasoning trajectories or textual responses. Recent evaluations cover diverse tasks but often rely on existing benchmarks, where prior exposure to test material can obscure generalization. Moreover, real-world decision making often involves long contexts, large option spaces, and complex logical dependencies, challenges that existing benchmarks do not adequately capture together. Thus, we introduce MMDM (Massive Multitask Decision Making), a benchmark of 5,448 high-quality questions across 16 task categories, combining newly constructed tasks with source-grounded recompositions and rewrites for single-select, multi-select, and soft-label decision making in fresh contexts. It covers realistic scenarios such as agent state tracking, tool and skill selection, preference judgments, and rubric scoring, with option spaces ranging from 2 to 1,024 and inputs extending to 64,817 tokens. With MMDM, we conduct a comprehensive quantitative evaluation of 28 decision models, showing that many perform well on common knowledge yet struggle with interdependent options, constrained decisions, and multi-select tasks. For example, Perplexity Decider-V1.2 reaches 78.75% Hard Single-select Accuracy on world knowledge, but only 53.95% on option logic and 47.95% on constraint planning, while the best Hard Multi-select Accuracy across all models is 36.81%. These results highlight joint reasoning, constraint-aware selection, and complete multi-select recovery as key priorities for decision model development, with MMDM providing a testbed for measuring this progress.

4. **DexForesight: Grounding Dexterous Policies with Implicit Spatial Foresight** - 👍 23
   - 기관: Sharpa1
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.11166)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11166)
   - Abstract: Dexterous manipulation requires rich spatial foresight over both the surrounding scene and the robot hand. Existing world-action models often acquire such predictive knowledge through explicit future prediction. We introduce DexForesight, a framework that learns spatial foresight implicitly by injecting dynamics-aware world representations into the latent space of a dexterous policy. During training, DexForesight learns an action-conditioned spatial world model from offline scene depth, kinematic hand geometry, and demonstration trajectories. The world model predicts 3D hand-point motion and visual feature changes, capturing both articulated hand dynamics and scene changes under demonstrated actions, including the motion of fingers occluded from the cameras. We then freeze the world model and align policy queries with its predictive world queries, transferring the learned spatial dynamics into policy latents and using them to condition action generation. At deployment, only the policy is retained, operating on RGB observations, language, and robot state without depth input or explicit future prediction. On 11 DexJoCo simulation tasks, DexForesight improves the overall success rate of the base policy from 49.4% to 60.5%. On three real-robot dexterous manipulation tasks, DexForesight achieves 58.3% success, compared with 50.0% for the strongest baseline. Project page this https URL

5. **DepthGraft: Structural Regularization through Hierarchical Cross-Layer KV Reconstruction** - 👍 12
   - 기관: dots studio91
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.03142)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03142)
   - Abstract: Scaling depth is a key route to stronger large language models, but Pre-LN architectures often suffer from depth-wise information dilution, limiting the effective use of deep layers. While cross-layer connectivity has proven effective at mitigating this problem, existing designs usually introduce additional computation or memory access. We revisit cross-layer KV sharing as an efficient form of such connectivity. Beyond cache compression, each KV reuse edge creates a direct gradient path from deep layers to source KV projections, turning KV sharing into an implicit structural regularizer for KV representation geometry. We provide a systematic analysis of this mechanism, showing that proper sharing topologies sharpen key retrieval directions while enriching the value content subspace, whereas existing adjacent or global-anchor designs do not fully exploit this effect. Motivated by this, we propose DepthGraft, a hierarchical KV-sharing architecture that reconstructs deep-layer KV from stride-aligned shallow-middle source pairs. DepthGraft distributes non-local gradient flow across source layers, while preserving prefill early completion and reducing KV cache by roughly one third. Experiments on dense and MoE models from 0.5B to 16B parameters show consistent downstream improvements under both GQA and MLA. Notably, on the 16B MoE model, DepthGraft achieves a 7.0% absolute improvement on MMLU-Pro, along with gains of 6.6% on CMMLU and 6.4% on C3. These results validate the potential of DepthGraft as a scalable design for large-scale pretraining. The project page is available at this https URL .

6. **Few-Step Streaming Video Generation via Condition Shifting** - 👍 12
   - 기관: Tsinghua University1
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.14033)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14033)
   - Abstract: Few-step sampling reduces the cost of streaming video generation. Distribution Matching Distillation (DMD) supervises few-step generators with a separate fake-score network, which adds training memory and computation and must track the evolving generator. We extend APEX to streaming video by using condition shifting to estimate the velocity field of the generated distribution within the generator. The original text condition selects generation, while a shifted condition selects fake estimation using the same parameters. This removes the independent fake network while retaining S2-Avatar's frozen reference teacher, Self-Replay Forcing, and perceptual regularization. We evaluate APEX on 180-second videos using three backbone evaluations per block and a one-step spatial Refiner, with a 70-case human study and a 320-case StreamAV-Bench comparison. Compared with S2-Avatar, APEX achieves higher video instruction fulfillment on the Progressive track (3.856 versus 3.831) and higher visual aesthetics across both tracks (0.641898 versus 0.632606). Human raters favor APEX most strongly on instruction following, with +30.0% net preference, followed by consistency and video quality at +4.3% each. Motion quality favors S2-Avatar, audiovisual synchronization is predominantly tied, and usability is unchanged. Separate measurements under matched training settings show 4.4% lower peak allocated GPU memory per GPU and 9.2% shorter mean iteration time than DMD.

7. **ProTEUS: A mutation-resolved world model for closed-loop protein evolution** - 👍 8
   - 기관: ·8 authors11
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.13348)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13348)
   - Abstract: Protein engineering requires navigating rugged fitness landscapes, where small sequence changes can produce large functional differences, but experimental screening can only sample a small fraction of sequence space. Thus, a central challenge is to efficiently traverse the large combinatorial space of mutations in silico. We introduce ProTEUS, a mutation-resolved world model for closed-loop protein evolution. Pretrained on deep mutational scanning assays, ProTEUS learns a joint representation of sequence, evolutionary, and structural information using MutationFormer, a mutation-biased attention architecture. Variants are represented as states in this joint latent space, and mutations define actions whose functional consequences are predicted. To support exploration of many mutants, we developed a fast, physics-constrained all-atom predictor that utilizes explicit geometric constraint enforcement to achieve >10 fold increase in speed than AlphaFold3 with guaranteed physical validity. Lastly, ProTEUS refines its prediction and understanding of protein activity through closed loop experimental feedback to guide evolution toward a desired phenotype. ProTEUS surpasses established models in both zero-shot and supervised fine-tuning mutation prediction tasks. We experimentally demonstrate its utility in two closed-loop multi-objective engineering campaigns on aldehyde dehydrogenase and levansucrase, yielding variants with >10 fold higher activity than wild type. ProTEUS provides a world-model framework that connects virtual evaluation of mutational outcomes with experimental learning for efficient protein evolution.

8. **PhysOPSD: Code as Privilege for Physically Plausible Video Generation** - 👍 8
   - 기관: TongyiLab11
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.12411)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12411)
   - Abstract: While modern video generative models achieve remarkable visual fidelity, they remain noticeably brittle when generating physically plausible dynamics and complex interactions. Targeted post-training is crucial, yet supervised fine-tuning suffers from offline state mismatch, and reinforcement learning is hindered by sparse, ill-defined physical reward functions. On-Policy (Self-)Distillation (OPSD) reconciles on-policy rollouts with dense corrective feedback, shifting the core challenge to designing effective privileged context. However, conventional modalities fall short: text prompts are inherently declarative, whereas real reference videos heavily entangle physical laws with visual appearance without parametric control. To this end, we propose PhysOPSD, introducing executable code as privileged physical context for video generation. Unlike static visual observations, executable code instantiates an editable physical world with intrinsic abstraction and controllability. Powered by PhysOPSD-Engine, we translate real-world physical scenarios into verifiable simulation programs. PhysOPSD then incorporates (i) dual-level physical privilege to bridge the information gap via an intermediate bottleneck, alongside (ii) controlled physical variations under shared rollout noise to expose causal dynamic shifts. Extensive experiments demonstrate that PhysOPSD substantially improves physical plausibility across diverse backbones, outperforming SFT and RL baselines with only $3$K samples, while preserving general video generation quality.

9. **Is Your Robot's Memory Just a Clock? Temporal Shortcuts in Memory-Augmented VLA Policies** - 👍 5
   - 기관: Nanyang Technological University11
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.14488)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14488)
   - Abstract: Memory-augmented vision-language-action (VLA) policies are meant to remember what happened. Many of them, however, fill memory by time: they sample frames uniformly across the episode and stamp each frame with its time step. Because time passes even when nothing happens, such a memory may not tell waiting from working. We show that it does not. On the counting tasks of RoboMME, we make the robot briefly pause or fidget its wrist. Such a null event changes nothing the task depends on, yet a released memory VLA counts the interval as work done and stops early. We call this failure phantom progress. It cuts success from 47 to 16 of 50 on one task and from 44 to 10 on the other. When the same frames are withheld from memory, success returns, so the cause lies in memory rather than in the interruption itself. We trace the failure to a temporal shortcut: because demonstrations proceed at a steady pace, elapsed time predicts progress. Indeed, rescaling the time stamps alone makes the policy stop early when the clock runs fast and repeat when it runs slow. Surprisingly, removing the time encoding does not cure the failure, because the frames of a null event still occupy slots of the time-sampled memory. Across RoboMME's released designs, vulnerability follows how memory is indexed. Of the fixes the diagnosis suggests, training with injected pauses does not remove the failure and impairs counting, whereas indexing memory by events recovers half or more of the loss without retraining. A memory meant to remember what happened should count events rather than clock ticks.

10. **EarthRecord: From Pixels to Land Change with Multimodal Agents over Four Decades of Satellite Imagery** - 👍 4
   - 기관: AIEyes Technologies (Hong Kong) Co., Limited01
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.12934)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12934)
   - Abstract: While multimodal agents now carry out complex data analysis with tools, their ability to turn decades of satellite imagery into reliable land-change analysis, crucial for Earth observation science, remains unmeasured. Existing benchmarks fall short: they pose questions on curated images or give agents preprocessed inputs, leaving out the raw multi-sensor archives where such analysis begins. We introduce EarthRecord, a benchmark built on the raw record of 56 sites: four decades of Landsat, MODIS, VIIRS, and Sentinel-2 data, with cloud masking and cross-sensor harmonization left to the agent. It poses 238 land-change questions across ten analysis tasks, scored against withheld references from global products, agreement among methods, and visual interpretation. Two controls test what answers rest on: the closed book removes the record, and instrument pairs alter one satellite's data while nothing changes on the ground. Evaluations of twelve multimodal agents reveal that leading agents complete most analyses end to end but falter when an answer requires mapping from imagery. Further analysis shows that the record moves answers from prior knowledge to the pixels but not yet to the real change on the ground: when one satellite's data are altered, half of the right answers follow the alteration. EarthRecord thus provides a principled platform for measuring and advancing agents from pixels to land change.
