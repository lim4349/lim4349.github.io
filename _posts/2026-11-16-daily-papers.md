1. **Gamer: A Generalist Vision-Language-Action Foundation Model for Long-Horizon Real-Time Game Agent** - 👍 58
   - 기관: ·27 authors1242
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.18397)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18397)
   - Abstract: We introduce Gamer, a generalist agent framework built for long-horizon tasks in complex interactive environments. Its end-to-end architecture combines perception, memory management, and multi-step reasoning. A shared training and deployment pipeline supports interaction with both conventional 2D graphical interfaces and complex 3D simulated worlds. We first continue pre-training on a dataset constructed from tens of thousands of hours of device-level keyboard and mouse interaction. These trajectories span simulated games and embodied environments, allowing the model to learn environmental dynamics across both physical and virtual domains. Sparse-thinking instruction tuning then substantially reduces redundant computation while preserving the reasoning needed for complex decisions. We train the policy for long-horizon interaction with a stable multi-turn reinforcement learning method that addresses finite context windows, accumulated rollout errors, and sparse rewards. For real-time interaction, a cloud-edge inference engine serves numerous simulated game environments concurrently, supports unbounded-context execution, and keeps online inference latency low. Across multiple challenging 3D open-world games, Gamer demonstrates solid generalization and operational efficiency comparable to skilled players. It also achieves competitive results on a range of conventional 2D game benchmarks. These results suggest that even repetitive keyboard and mouse trajectories can support substantial gains from scaling data and learning algorithms. Training on these trajectories helps Gamer learn complex skills more steadily in environments with both physical and digital properties. This approach offers a path toward generalist agents that can interact across digital and physical worlds.

2. **Envisioning Agentic Robotics: A New Era of Robotic Systems** - 👍 50
   - 기관: ·13 authors2
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.18359)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18359)
   - Abstract: The release of GPT-6 Astra marks the emergence of a new paradigm in robotics: agentic robotics. Within weeks, frontier models with agentic harnesses matched or exceeded task-specific policies on many embodied benchmarks. Instead of training a dedicated policy for each family of tasks, an agentic robotic system applies a foundation model to open-world embodied tasks. It operates in a closed loop: observing the environment, interpreting user intent, reasoning about the task, invoking skills and tools, verifying outcomes, and adapting its strategy. Despite these advances, the community has yet to converge on a shared definition, a reliable evaluation framework, or a research roadmap. We argue that Embodied System 2, realized by a foundation model together with an embodied harness, forms the decision-making core of agentic robotics. Its central challenge is to translate general-purpose reasoning into effective and verifiable actions under physical constraints such as limited reaction time, mechanical limits, unpredictable environment changes, and irreversible consequences. Motivated by this perspective, we propose an evaluation framework that measures both model capabilities and closed-loop system performance. Furthermore, we identify the principles that carry over from digital to physical agents and the challenges that must be addressed to achieve reliable physical autonomy. On top of the Embodied System 2 and the evaluation framework, we envision a research roadmap toward sustained autonomy: the ability to perform work safely and reliably with progressively broader responsibility, from executing individual primitives to fulfilling standing roles.

3. **MMFlash: Faster Speculative Decoding for Vision-Language Models** - 👍 25
   - 기관: National University of Singapore131
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.18482)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18482)
   - Abstract: Speculative decoding accelerates generation by letting a lightweight drafter propose tokens for parallel verification by a target model. Diffusion drafters such as DFlash propose an entire token block in a single forward pass, but mainly focus on text-only tasks, leaving multimodal drafting underexplored. To study this setting, we first introduce a metric of token-level visual dependence based on the KL divergence between next-token distributions with and without visual input. We find that acceptance rates decrease from 46% in the lowest visual-dependence quartile to 22% in the highest, with this disparity persisting after multimodal training. Motivated by this observation, we propose MMFlash, a simple and effective framework that adaptively upweights visually dependent tokens that the drafter predicts poorly. For long video inputs, MMFlash further employs sparse attention to reduce redundant attention computation and improve drafting quality. Across various multimodal benchmarks, MMFlash achieves lossless decoding speedups of up to $3.58\times$, outperforming EAGLE-3 and DFlash by up to 195.4% and 17.1%, respectively.

4. **JVT: Just Video Transformer makes pixel-space video diffusion simple** - 👍 13
   - 기관: ·3 authors41
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.18549)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18549)
   - Abstract: Direct diffusion modeling of high-dimensional raw video data is challenging, and existing pixel-space methods often rely on specialized architectures. We introduce JVT (Just Video Transformer), a simple large-patch DiT-style Transformer for pixel-space video diffusion, without a pretrained autoencoder, auxiliary detailers, cascaded stages, or U-Net-style skip connections. We systematically examine video patch embeddings, register tokens, and adaptive layer normalization to identify design choices that balance generation quality and computational efficiency. The resulting JVT design achieves generation quality competitive with latent diffusion baselines in both class-conditioned and unconditional settings. The pixel-space formulation of JVT also naturally supports joint generation of pixel-aligned modalities. We demonstrate this capability through camera-conditioned RGB-disparity generation, which improves camera following relative to RGB-only baselines and yields explicit scene geometry without an additional reconstruction model at inference. These results highlight the potential of simple DiT-style Transformers for pixel-space video diffusion, and we hope our findings offer useful insights and practical guidance for future research.

5. **MEM-SQL: Memory-Empowered Text-to-SQL** - 👍 13
   - 기관: The University of Hong Kong1
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.18338)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18338)
   - Abstract: Text-to-SQL systems translate natural language questions into SQL queries over increasingly complex databases. However, widely used benchmarks evaluate questions independently, overlooking settings where earlier queries over the same database can supply joins or calculations for a new question. We introduce HARE (History-AwaRe Text-to-SQL BEnchmark) to measure the accuracy and cost of adapting historical SQL. HARE comprises 1,600 requests derived from a fixed pool of 823 verified queries across four datasets. Annotations record which components each request preserves and which edits it requires, distinguishing whole-query adaptation from partial reuse. Our method, MEM-SQL (Memory-EMpowered Text-to-SQL), stores whole-query templates, reusable SQL blocks, and mappings from domain concepts to schema elements. MEM-SQL uses a layer only if every requirement assigned to it has a match above that layer's threshold. With On-demand Schema Linking (OSL), MEM-SQL consults the schema only when no layer qualifies or the generator judges retrieved evidence insufficient. Across six language models on HARE, mean execution accuracy rises from 37.1% for Direct Generation without history to 50.0% for MEM-SQL, with 76.6% fewer input tokens and 68.8% lower latency on average. Further analysis shows that requests reusing several verified queries remain a bottleneck, with accuracy falling from 56.7% to 29.7%, most sharply when reused components must be placed in a new query structure or combined with a shared join. Applying OSL to three other memory designs consistently reduces generation cost, while its accuracy benefit depends on the memory design. Verified history can thus make generation both more accurate and cheaper, but its value depends on the evidence retained and when the system consults the schema.

6. **Loopback Transformer: Towards a Unified View of Residual Looped Transformers** - 👍 13
   - 기관: University of Wisconsin - Madison31
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.07226)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07226)
   - Abstract: Looped Transformers reduce parameter count by repeatedly applying shared blocks, but parameter sharing alone does not determine how the results of repeated computation are reused. We take the depth-memory view of residual connections and analyze which representation is stored after each block application and how later reads weight it. A full block output contains both its input and its new update, so weighting that output also reweights the history already carried by the input. Reading individual updates instead, with the shared stack's input kept on a separate path, gives separate controls over input strength, total update weight, and allocation across updates. The Loopback Transformer implements these controls: geometric decay sets the total update weight and supplies a recency prior, and attention learns how to allocate that weight. On ClimbMix, Loopback reaches lower validation loss than standard Transformer baselines with 40-75% of their Transformer parameters (excluding token embeddings and the output head) at reference scales from 191M to 3B, with training tokens and executed depth matched. It also has higher mean accuracy over thirteen downstream tasks at 1.4B and 3B. At 191M, 374M, and 778M, it has the lowest validation loss and the highest thirteen-task mean among the standard Transformer and seven looped residual designs.

7. **Rethinking in Spikes: Mitigating Hallucinations in MDLMs with Step-Aware Decoding** - 👍 11
   - 기관: Cornell University1
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.12901)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12901)
   - Abstract: Recent advancements in multimodal diffusion language models (MDLMs) have exhibited strong global modeling capabilities through iterative refinement. We observe that mask uncertainty follows an overall downward trend during iterative denoising, while a few denoising steps exhibit localized entropy spikes closely associated with hallucinations. We argue that anomalous steps can be identified by measuring the entropy deviation of each denoising step from its neighboring temporal window. We hypothesize that discrete token commitment discards the distributional information at masked positions, limiting the continued interaction between candidate semantics and the context during entropy spikes. With this goal, we present StepRefine, a plug-and-play decoding strategy that leverages semantic context to achieve reliable denoising. The core of our method lies in a step-aware switch between discrete denoising and latent refinement. The model employs probability-weighted embeddings at entropy spike steps to preserve diverse reasoning hypotheses and performs multiple rounds of continuous refinement. Meanwhile, a refinement controller monitors distributional stability to reduce unnecessary loops and switch the model back to standard discrete denoising. Through extensive experiments, StepRefine demonstrates significant hallucination mitigation across different MDLMs on multimodal benchmarks.

8. **HALO: Human-Aligned Data Collection for WhoLe-body MObile Manipulation** - 👍 11
   - 기관: Robbyant Research11
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.16439)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16439)
   - Abstract: Learning whole-body mobile manipulation requires demonstrations that coordinate bimanual actions with locomotion across extended workspaces. However, existing interfaces trade off direct robot control, perception and action alignment, and natural operator mobility, making such demonstrations difficult to collect. We introduce HALO, a human-aligned data collection system for whole-body mobile manipulation. The system aligns the operator's perception and action space with the robot while preserving direct physical feedback and natural locomotion. Operators guide the robot end-effectors through kinesthetic teaching and move a low-resistance platform without the deployed mobile base while performing bimanual manipulation. This interface enables more natural whole-body demonstrations, but the close human-robot coupling introduces discrepancies between the demonstration and deployment settings. We address these discrepancies with deployment-aligned demonstration processing, which calibrates and temporally aligns platform motion with the deployed base response and masks and inpaints demonstrator hands in recorded observations. Compared with Mobile ALOHA and VR teleoperation, HALO enables up to 2.9x faster demonstration collection while maintaining comparable or higher success rates. Policies trained with HALO perform precise manipulation tasks, including zip-tie fastening, and achieve a 73.3% success rate on a six-stage long-horizon mobile manipulation task involving repeated coordinated base and arm motion. 9. **JFinBench: A Continuously Refreshable Japanese Financial Benchmark from the Latest Certification Exams** - 👍 9
   - 기관: Sakana AI01
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.18130)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18130)
   - Abstract: Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly applied to finance, creating a need for benchmarks that evaluate their capabilities across financial systems with different regulations and practices. However, existing financial benchmarks provide limited coverage of country-specific financial systems, and they are difficult to keep up to date as released questions leak into training data. We introduce JFinBench, a Japanese financial benchmark comprising 321 examples from the 2026 editions of six regularly administered certification exams covering accounting, tax, financial planning, and banking. To construct it, we develop a human-in-the-loop agentic pipeline in which a coding agent writes exam-specific conversion code and a review page, while a human verifies every example and feeds corrections back to the agent. This pipeline adapts to heterogeneous exam formats and reduces the effort of incorporating new exam editions, enabling periodic refreshes that mitigate data contamination. Evaluating 12 recent LLMs, we find that Claude Fable 5 and GPT-6 Astra exceed 93% overall, while even the best open-weight model, Kimi K3.1 Pro, trails Claude Fable 5 by 12 percentage points. When models can additionally use a terminal and web access, they retrieve the knowledge they lack and every model improves. The gap narrows but about 4 points remain, and the ranking of models stays largely the same, suggesting that knowledge held in the model itself remains valuable even with tools. We release the dataset, the evaluation code, and the construction pipeline.

10. **AI Reviews Are Biased Towards the Appendix** - 👍 9
   - 기관: CincyNLP Lab @ University of Cincinnati01
   - [HF 페이지](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.17811)
   - [논문 링크](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17811)
   - Abstract: Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly used by reviewers to highlight weaknesses during the reviewing process, and their outputs often shape the reviews that humans submit. Prior work studies how LLM reviews align with human ones, yet we know little about which parts of a paper these models actually criticize. In this work, we investigate 2,000 papers from ACL, EMNLP, COLM, and ICLR, and show that the latest GPT and Claude models draw about 50% or more of their review weaknesses from the appendix. This behavior holds across the API, coding-agent, and web access methods for using LLMs to review a paper. In contrast, we find that humans draw only 8.4% of their weaknesses from the appendix, and these weaknesses primarily revolve around a paper's presentation and content related to its core idea. Meanwhile, LLMs draw weaknesses from deep in the appendix that concern fine-grained technical details not central to the paper's contributions.
